We run this mic as a safer and more welcoming space, especially for queer folks and women. Everyone is welcome when they can respect the room, the lineup, and each other.

Event Rules & Expectations

For Performers
Performers of all identities are welcome to perform, but please be respectful of the audience and the room.
Harassment, hate speech, or targeted cruelty toward individuals based on their identity is not allowed. Don't be a jerk.
Punching down is not the vibe. Challenge systems, not people with less power.
Keep stage and floor interactions consensual. Do not touch audience members or performers without clear permission.
Crowdwork is allowed, but if you are asked to stop, please do so immediately.
If you are a performer and you are uncomfortable with a joke, please let the host know immediately.
Respect your set length and the host's timing calls so everyone gets a fair shot.
Hosts may cut sets or skip performers if rules are ignored. Repeat violations can mean removal from future mics.
For Audience Members
Be kind and present. Listen while performers are on stage and keep side conversations to a minimum.
No heckling, harassment, or threatening behavior.
Honor names, pronouns, and boundaries. If someone tells you a boundary, respect it immediately.
If something feels unsafe, find the host or organizer right away. We will listen and act.
Accountability & Safety
Organizers can warn, remove, or deny entry to anyone who violates these expectations.
Safety calls are made to protect the room, this is a safe space first and foremost. We appreciate your cooperation.
If you need to report an issue privately, email us at mercymecomedy@gmail.com.
